Empirical evidence

Keep the result, even when it is inconvenient.

CogniPrint's evidence layer exists to make it harder to blur measurements, inference and claims. The fixed Stage A pilot is therefore a central result precisely because the compact profile did not win.

Fixed Stage A · Accuracy

0.5973 > 0.5839 > 0.5369 > 0.2953

Word TF-IDF, character TF-IDF, CogniPrint 12D, length-only.

Fixed Stage A · Macro-F1

0.5952 > 0.5788 > 0.5359 > 0.2474

Same ordering. The current 12D nearest-centroid profile is not the strongest classifier on this controlled task.

Scientific consequence: the result redirects the research question away from “a compact profile must be a superior classifier” and toward stability, transformations, uncertainty, baselines and provenance.
