Evidence ontology

Four states. None is optional.

OBSERVED

Direct measurements from the text or artifact: profile coordinates, hashes, measured distances, perturbation deltas.

INFERRED

A conclusion conditional on a stated reference set, model, calibration procedure, or comparison rule.

ATTESTED

External authenticated provenance such as signatures, content credentials, trusted logs, or repository/publication records.

UNKNOWN

The evidence does not justify a stronger statement. Uncertainty is a first-class output, not an error state.
