01 · Origin

The project started with measurement, not detection.

The original question was simple enough to write as a map: can a text be represented as a compact point in a measurable space, and can controlled edits be studied as movement in that space?

HYPOTHESIS

Text has measurable structure.

Represent empirical regularities without pretending that a statistical profile is a biological identity.

THEORY

Map text into a finite-dimensional space.

Define interpretable coordinates, distances, similarity and perturbation operators.

PROGRAM

Make the mathematics executable.

Implement deterministic extraction, corpus analysis, experiments, evidence artifacts and reproducibility tooling.

TEST

Let baselines challenge the idea.

Freeze a leakage-safe pilot and retain negative results when simpler methods win.

EXPAND

Move from profiles to evidence.

Study provenance, uncertainty and portable evidence in more complex AI workflows without erasing the mathematical origin.

02 · Mathematical core

From a text to a point in ℝ¹².

φ(T) = [f₁(T), f₂(T), …, f₁₂(T)] ∈ ℝ¹²

A compact profile of interpretable empirical statistics. The map supports comparison and perturbation studies; it is not a proof of identity or origin.

Entropy

H(X) = −Σ p(x) log₂ p(x)

Distance

d₂(a,b) = √Σ(aᵢ−bᵢ)²

Similarity

cos(a,b) = (a·b)/(‖a‖‖b‖)

Perturbation

Δφ = φ(T′) − φ(T)

05 · Broader AI evidence research

From transformation traces to Portable Evidence Capsules.

The later programme extends the same discipline into multi-principal agent workflows: preserve provenance, failure traces and uncertainty without claiming more than the evidence supports.

Provenance

Event, delegation, version, authorization and lineage evidence across independently controlled systems.

Failure tracing

Locate broken action paths without automatically assigning responsibility to the wrong principal.

Privacy

Explore verification from hashes, bounded metadata and structured records when raw payloads cannot be shared.

Uncertainty

Preserve UNKNOWN and abstention when records are incomplete, conflicting or insufficient.

06 · Scientific boundary

Evidence before verdict.

Supported today

  • Descriptive statistical profiles
  • Controlled transformations and comparisons
  • Entropy and n-gram diagnostics
  • Versioned evidence artifacts
  • Explicit uncertainty states

Not established

  • Author identity or intent
  • Unique or exact source model from text similarity
  • Definitive AI origin
  • Generation-lineage reconstruction from text alone
  • Legal responsibility or forensic provenance
