Reviewer context

Current claim posture

Supported

Descriptive profiles, controlled transformations, entropy/n-gram diagnostics, evidence artifacts and explicit uncertainty states.

Not claimed

Authorship, exact model identity, definitive AI origin, legal responsibility, forensic identity or a universal high-stakes detector.

Current scientific readiness: descriptive_only. A stronger public claim should require evidence appropriate to that claim.

Suggested review questions

  1. Are the 12 profile coordinates defined clearly enough to reproduce?
  2. Are scaling, distance and similarity choices scientifically justified for the intended descriptive role?
  3. Do perturbation experiments distinguish stability from attribution?
  4. Are the Stage A baselines sufficient to falsify simple explanations?
  5. Are UNKNOWN/OOD/calibration gates strong enough before any future attribution claim?
  6. Does the evidence architecture keep statistical inference separate from external provenance?
