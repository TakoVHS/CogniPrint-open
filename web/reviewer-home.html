Multi-Agent AI Safety Research

Portable Evidence Capsules for Multi-Principal Agent Workflows

Multi-principal provenance

Represent event, delegation, version, authorization, and lineage evidence across independently controlled systems.

Failure tracing

Test whether evidence can localize a broken delegation or action path without assigning responsibility to the wrong principal.

Privacy-preserving verification

Evaluate what can be verified from hashes, bounded metadata, and structured records when raw payloads cannot be shared.

Fail-closed uncertainty

Preserve explicit UNKNOWN and abstention states when records are missing, conflicting, replayed, reordered, or insufficient.
