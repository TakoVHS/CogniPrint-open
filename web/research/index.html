Research programme

From a mathematical hypothesis to an evidence programme.

CogniPrint is organized as an evidence-gated research programme: theory first, executable measurement second, controlled experiments third, claims only after the relevant evidence survives.

T → φ(T) → Δφ → evidence → uncertainty

Current public readiness remains descriptive_only. Stage B of Attribution Challenge 001 is not authorized to start.

01

Hypothesis

Statistical regularities in text can be represented reproducibly without turning a profile into a unique identity claim.

02

Mathematical model

Define an interpretable 12-dimensional feature map, distance/similarity measures and perturbation operators.

03

Executable framework

Implement deterministic feature extraction, corpus summaries, perturbation tools, entropy and n-gram diagnostics.

04

Controlled empirical campaigns

Measure how profiles move under bounded edits and transformations, preserving campaign metadata and evidence artifacts.

05

Leakage-safe baselines

Compare compact profiles against transparent baselines; preserve the Stage A result in which TF-IDF outperforms the current 12D classifier.

06

Evidence architecture

Separate OBSERVED, INFERRED, ATTESTED and UNKNOWN states.

07

Challenge 001

Pre-freeze work on blinded evaluation, OOD/UNKNOWN handling, calibration, leakage controls and falsification criteria. The blind Stage B remains gated.

08

Broader provenance research

Portable Evidence Capsules, multi-principal workflows, privacy-preserving verification and failure tracing extend the evidence discipline beyond text-only measurement.

Research rule

A result may unlock a claim. A convenient story may not.

Reproducibility

Exact software revisions, data lineage, deterministic artifacts and explicit execution evidence.

Falsification

Negative results and baseline wins remain part of the public research record.

Abstention

UNKNOWN is a first-class research output when evidence is insufficient.
