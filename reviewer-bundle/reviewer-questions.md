# CogniPrint Reviewer Bundle — Reviewer Questions

Please answer only what you can assess. A short critical response is acceptable. Endorsement is not requested.

## A. Descriptive methodology

1. Are the mathematical objects and assumptions defined clearly enough for independent review?
2. Are coordinate-stability and non-degeneracy assumptions appropriately bounded?
3. Are perturbation/stability statements correctly framed as conditional?
4. Are Euclidean distance and cosine similarity suitable descriptive tools at the current release stage?

## B. Reproducibility and evidence custody

5. Is the source/features/selection/source-contract hash chain adequate for a development benchmark?
6. Is the raw-text privacy boundary compatible with independent verification?
7. Does the byte-identical persisted M2 result across two external workflow runs provide adequate engineering reproducibility evidence?
8. What additional artifact should be public before external scientific replication?

## C. Executed RAID M1/M2

M1 and M2 are now executed Stage A development evidence. They are not sealed Stage B attribution results.

9. Is the M1 negative baseline comparison interpreted appropriately?
10. Is the M2 five-role split adequate to avoid train/calibration/threshold/evaluation leakage?
11. Is leave-one-machine-family-out useful as development open-set evidence even though every family is known to the research programme?
12. Is treating cosine-centroid score vectors as logit-like inputs to scalar temperature scaling defensible?
13. What should be done about all n-gram temperatures hitting the lower bound `T=0.05` without post-result tuning?
14. Does the deterioration of 12D held-out NLL after temperature scaling falsify the current calibration hypothesis strongly enough?
15. Is AURC being interpreted narrowly enough given full-coverage performance differences?
16. Is the max-confidence false-known/AUROC result sufficient to reject that score as a useful OOD baseline here?
17. Should unknown conformal empty sets and ambiguous sets be reported separately before any OOD language is used?
18. What cross-fold estimand/inference method, if any, is defensible given dependence across leave-one-family-out folds?
19. Does the current evidence justify keeping 12D only as an interpretable baseline rather than pursuing it as an attribution representation?
20. What result should force abandonment of the current model-family fingerprint hypothesis entirely?

## E. Human–AI intervention research

29. What negative result should cause this direction to be abandoned or substantially narrowed?

## F. Provenance and safety

30. Is the distinction between content-derived measurements and authenticated provenance strong enough?
31. Which provenance sources should outrank content-derived signals?
32. How should disagreement between content signals and signed/declared provenance be represented?
33. Are non-claims around authorship, exact model identity, AI origin, actor/commissioner identity, legal status and forensic provenance sufficiently clear?
34. Which false-positive or overconfidence failure modes are most serious?
35. Does any part of the public package still overclaim?

## Review classification

Please indicate whether your response is:

- substantive methodological review;
- informal methodological comment;
- decline/referral;
- acknowledgement only.

Only a substantive independent methodological assessment may be counted toward the external review counter. A critical or negative review can satisfy that definition.
