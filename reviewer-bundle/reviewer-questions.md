# CogniPrint Reviewer Bundle — Reviewer Questions

Please answer only what you can assess. A short critical response is acceptable. Endorsement is not requested.

## A. Current descriptive methodology

1. Are the mathematical objects and assumptions defined clearly enough for independent review?
2. Are the coordinate-stability and non-degeneracy assumptions appropriately bounded?
3. Are the perturbation/stability statements correctly framed as conditional?
4. Are Euclidean distance and cosine similarity suitable descriptive tools for this release stage?

## B. Current empirical protocol

5. Is the public diagnostic layer sufficient for a `descriptive_only` release?
6. Are benchmark provenance and licensing notes clear enough?
7. Are the reproduction instructions adequate for independent inspection?
8. Which simple baselines or negative controls are missing from the current descriptive package?

## C. Model-family benchmark design

The repository now contains a proposed external RAID pilot. No RAID attribution result is currently claimed.

9. Is the proposed model/domain/decoding control sufficient to make a first benchmark interpretable?
10. Does the lineage-group split adequately guard against prompt/source leakage? What additional grouping is necessary?
11. Are majority, length-only, n-gram, and current 12D fingerprint baselines sufficient as a first comparison set?
12. What experiment would most convincingly falsify the model-family fingerprint hypothesis?
13. Which open-world or unseen-model tests are required before even a benchmark-bounded attribution statement is responsible?
14. How should calibration and abstention be evaluated before any source-family suggestion is shown to a user?

## D. Human–AI intervention research

15. Is sentence/span-level localisation a scientifically meaningful target under realistic human editing, or is the proposed construct too ambiguous?
16. What ground-truth process would be credible for substantial human intervention labels?
17. Which revision-chain, context-window, translation, or paraphrase confounders must be controlled?
18. What negative result should cause CogniPrint to abandon or substantially narrow this research direction?

## E. Provenance and evidence boundary

19. Is the distinction between content-derived measurements and authenticated provenance stated strongly enough?
20. Which provenance sources should outrank content-derived signals in an evidence workflow?
21. How should the system represent disagreement between content signals and signed/declared provenance?
22. Are the non-claims around authorship, exact model identity, AI origin, actor/commissioner identity, legal status, and forensic provenance sufficiently clear?

## F. Safety, misuse, and external validity

23. Which false-positive or overconfidence failure modes are most serious?
24. Which languages/domains or transformations would make current results misleading if omitted?
25. Does any part of the public package appear to overclaim?
26. What risks or limitations should be added before grant-funded expansion?

## Review classification

Please indicate whether your response is:

- substantive methodological review;
- informal methodological comment;
- decline/referral;
- acknowledgement only.

Only a substantive independent methodological assessment may be counted toward the external review counter. A critical or negative review can satisfy that definition; agreement with CogniPrint is not required.
