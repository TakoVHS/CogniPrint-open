# Primary Submission Target — Evidence-Bounded Promotion

Status: **PRIMARY TARGET SELECTED / NO SUBMISSION SENT**

## Primary target

**Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)**

Rationale:

- TMLR uses rolling submission rather than a one-shot conference deadline.
- Its stated editorial emphasis is technical correctness rather than subjective
  significance.
- Its scope explicitly includes application papers, formalizations of new
  learning tasks or assessment methods, and analytical frameworks.
- This matches the current paper's honest position: a methodological
  certificate-to-claim framework with an AI-attribution application, not a
  claim of a new probability theory or type theory.
- TMLR is archival and citable, which better matches the immediate goal of a
  first durable publication than relying on a workshop that may be non-archival.

## Submission constraints to satisfy before submission

1. Port the manuscript to the mandatory TMLR LaTeX style/template.
2. Keep the submitted PDF double blind.
3. Verify that every author has a complete active OpenReview profile.
4. Add the required first-page disclosure that an LLM was used as an
   assistive tool; the human author remains responsible for all claims,
   derivations, citations, and text.
5. Keep novelty claims narrow and support every mathematical claim with either
   a proof or an explicit citation.
6. Complete the full-text prior-art comparison, especially the Zhu preprint,
   before finalizing the Relation to Prior Work section.
7. Do not dual-submit substantially overlapping work to another archival
   peer-reviewed venue while the TMLR submission is active.
8. Keep Stage B outside the paper unless and until separately preregistered and
   authorized.

## Why no 2027 workshop is selected today

As of 24 November 2026, the ICLR 2027 workshop program has not yet been
finalized: the official workshop acceptance notification is scheduled for
29 November 2026. Individual workshop calls therefore cannot yet be treated as
stable submission targets merely because proposal websites exist.

ICML 2027 has published its main-conference dates, but the 2027 individual
workshop calls are not yet available. The official main-track policy has also
changed materially: ICML 2027 now explicitly supports contribution types such
as Negative Results, Application-driven, and Concept and Feasibility. That
makes the main track more plausible than older assumptions suggested, but it
is not selected as the primary target here.
