## Evidence obtained

### Confirmed from the Zenodo public record

The public record states that:

- validation observes proxy evidence rather than the deployment target
  directly;
- certification is defined over worlds compatible with a declared evidence
  protocol ("evidence fibers");
- the work computes sharp target images / envelopes;
- it derives exact envelopes for hidden representation-induced channels;
- it identifies minimal audits and coarsest refinements that close residual
  uncertainty;
- it compiles positive residual width into executable finite-horizon
  counterworlds.

These points are a direct collision with any broad CogniPrint claim to have
invented compatible-world semantics, sharp target envelopes, evidence
refinement, or counterworld witnesses.

### Confirmed from the reproducibility archive preview

The public ZIP preview exposes a reproducibility package containing, among
other items:

- \`source/main.tex\`;
- \`source/theory/fiber_channel_theorem_package.tex\`;
- \`run_exact_envelope_sweep.py\`;
- \`run_audit_repair_curve.py\`;
- \`run_no_promotion_partial_audit.py\`;
- \`run_fiber_worldmodel_witness.py\`;
- result files for exact envelopes, no-promotion partial audits, audit repair
  curves, and executable witness constructions.

This materially increases the collision risk beyond what can be inferred from
the title alone.
