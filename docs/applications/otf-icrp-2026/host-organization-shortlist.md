# OTF ICRP 2026 — Host Organization Shortlist

> Research-fit assessment only. **No organization below has agreed to host, supervise, endorse, fund, or partner with CogniPrint.** No affiliation is implied.

## Host requirements from OTF

For the ICRP research period, the proposed host should be able to support a full-time researcher, provide meaningful oversight, and contribute relevant expertise. OTF permits academic or non-academic hosts, including organizations outside the United States, and allows remote arrangements when a workable communication strategy exists.

For this project, the ideal host adds capabilities the applicant should not self-certify:

- internet-freedom and information-controls expertise;
- methodological challenge and research supervision;
- ethics/data-safety review;
- familiarity with censorship/content moderation, digital threats or measurement research;
- a named adviser able to conduct monthly progress review;
- practitioner/community context for the outputs;
- willingness to support a remote researcher based in Viet Nam if necessary.

## Tier 1 — strongest thematic fit

### 1. The Citizen Lab, University of Toronto

**Why it fits**

Citizen Lab conducts interdisciplinary research on digital threats to human rights and has explicit focus areas in **Artificial Intelligence** and **Censorship**. Its censorship work includes technical research into online censorship and content moderation, and its researchers have substantial experience in information controls and digital repression. The Lab also has Southeast Asia expertise, including research on how governments regulate and impose controls on online information flows.

**What it could add**

- strong information-controls methodology;
- content-moderation/censorship framing;
- ethics and human-rights research discipline;
- Southeast Asia context;
- experienced review of claims that could otherwise be overinterpreted.

**Main concern**

High-demand academic environment; host availability must not be assumed. The Stage 2 outreach should ask about fit and supervision capacity, not present the organization as a planned partner before consent.

Official references:

- https://citizenlab.ca/research/
- https://citizenlab.ca/focus-area/censorship/
- https://citizenlab.ca/who-we-are/

**Readiness rank:** `A`

## Tier 2 — strong measurement/reproducibility fit

### 2. Open Observatory of Network Interference (OONI)

**Why it fits**

OONI is a global community and open measurement project focused on internet censorship. It maintains open measurement software, datasets and research workflows used across many countries.

**What it could add**

- mature measurement methodology;
- open-data and reproducibility practice;
- strong internet-freedom community connection;
- useful challenge on how to frame measurement evidence and uncertainty.

**Main concern**

OONI's primary technical domain is network interference, while this proposal studies AI-mediated text transformations and information controls above the network layer. The host case therefore has to be framed around measurement methodology, evidence quality and practitioner utility rather than pretending the research is ordinary network-censorship measurement.

Official references:

- https://ooni.org/
- https://ooni.org/data/

**Readiness rank:** `A-`

### 3. Censored Planet, University of Michigan

**Why it fits**

Censored Planet is a longitudinal censorship-measurement research platform with a strong record in reproducible, large-scale measurement, false-positive control and public research data.

**What it could add**

- rigorous measurement design;
- longitudinal/drift thinking;
- false-positive and anomaly methodology;
- reproducibility and benchmark review;
- direct alignment with OTF's information-controls research ecosystem.

**Main concern**

Its core research is primarily network-level censorship measurement. As with OONI, the fit is methodological rather than a direct overlap with multilingual AI transformation research.

Official references:

- https://censoredplanet.org/censoredplanet
- https://censoredplanet.org/publications

**Readiness rank:** `A-`

## Tier 3 — strongest practitioner/community bridge

### 4. Internews — Internet Freedom / digital-security research ecosystem

**Why it fits**

Internews supports civil society and media through internet-freedom, digital-security, threat-analysis and incident-response work. Its practitioner-facing materials are designed for civil society, journalists and human-rights defenders.

**What it could add**

- practitioner relevance and user-needs review;
- at-risk community perspective;
- digital-evidence and incident-response context;
- dissemination and guidance that practitioners can actually use.

**Main concern**

The research-method supervision fit must be confirmed for this specific AI-mediated information-controls project; the organization should not be selected solely because of broad internet-freedom alignment.

Official reference:

- https://internews.org/resource/field-guide-to-incident-response-for-civil-society-and-media/

**Readiness rank:** `B+`

## Selection matrix

A candidate host should be evaluated on six dimensions before outreach is treated as successful:

1. thematic fit with information controls;
2. methodological supervision capacity;
3. multilingual / regional relevance;
4. practitioner access and impact;
5. remote monthly oversight capacity;
6. conflict-of-interest / independence / compliance fit.

The preferred host is not necessarily the most prestigious institution. The strongest choice is the organization that can materially improve the research and reliably perform OTF's monthly oversight role.

3. Censored Planet;
4. Internews or another OTF-compatible practitioner-focused host identified with OTF.

This ordering is a readiness recommendation only. **No outreach is authorized by this document.**

## Host outreach packet to prepare

The first host message should be accompanied by a concise, factual package containing:

- one-page research summary;
- 12-month workplan;
- expected host responsibilities and monthly cadence;
- why the specific host is relevant;
- public repository and research-boundary links;
- explicit statement that the application is pending and no funding/award is claimed;
- request for a brief fit conversation rather than an immediate endorsement.

## Host confirmation gate

`HOST_CONFIRMED=YES` only after a named authorized contact explicitly confirms willingness to host/supervise under a structure compatible with OTF. A friendly reply, generic interest, referral, or unanswered outreach does not satisfy this gate.
