## Subject

Potential OTF ICRP host fit — multilingual AI-mediated information-controls research

## Core email draft

Dear [Name / Team],

My name is Roman Adriashkin. I am an independent researcher-engineer and the founder of the open-source CogniPrint Research Initiative.

I have submitted a concept note to the Open Technology Fund's 2026 Information Controls Research Program (ICRP) for a proposed 12-month project titled **“Evidence After the Filter: Privacy-Preserving Measurement of AI-Mediated Information Controls in Vietnamese and Russian-Language Workflows.”**

The proposed research examines how AI-assisted translation, summarization, rewriting/paraphrase, and moderation/refusal workflows may alter sensitive information across Vietnamese, Russian, and English contexts. The project is designed to measure observable effects such as omission, sanitization, semantic drift, compression, uncertainty changes, and loss of provenance context, while developing privacy-aware evidence records that can be independently reviewed without turning text similarity into attribution or identity claims.

If the application advances to OTF's full-proposal stage, I will need to identify a host organization able to provide substantive research oversight during the project. I am contacting you because [one sentence specific to the organization and its actual research/work].

The expected host role would be research supervision rather than financial sponsorship: a named adviser, regular methodological review, monthly progress oversight compatible with OTF's ICRP process, and guidance on ethics/data safety and practitioner relevance. The work can be structured remotely if appropriate.

The current project status is deliberately conservative: CogniPrint's public scientific readiness remains `descriptive_only`; the project does not claim authorship identification, exact-model identification, legal/forensic provenance, actor responsibility, or universal AI-origin detection.

If this falls within your interests and hosting capacity, I would be grateful for a short conversation about fit. I can provide the one-page research summary, 12-month workplan, risk/ethics plan, and public repository for review. I completely understand if your organization is unable to host the project.

Kind regards,

## Fit-call agenda

If a candidate responds positively, the first call should resolve:

1. Is the research within the organization's substantive remit?
2. Is there a named adviser with capacity for monthly supervision?
3. Can the organization support OTF's host oversight/traffic-light role?
4. Can remote full-time work from Viet Nam be supported operationally?
5. What ethics/data-safety process would apply?
6. Are there restrictions on public release, practitioner engagement, or sensitive data handling?
7. Are there conflict-of-interest, funding, legal, or administrative constraints?
8. What in-kind resources, if any, would be available and must be disclosed to OTF?
9. Who is authorized to confirm host willingness in writing?

## Confirmation language

Do not set `HOST_CONFIRMED=YES` from a friendly or exploratory reply. Confirmation requires explicit written agreement from an authorized representative that the organization is willing to serve as the proposed ICRP host under terms compatible with OTF, subject to any stated conditions.
