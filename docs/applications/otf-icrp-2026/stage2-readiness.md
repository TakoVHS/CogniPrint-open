## Scientific and claim boundary

Nothing in this Stage 2 package may state or imply that CogniPrint currently establishes:

- authorship or intent;
- exact model identity from text similarity;
- legal or forensic provenance;
- responsibility of a person or organization;
- universal AI-origin detection;
- completed results for future ICRP experiments.

The funded-research hypothesis is narrower: AI-assisted translation, summarization, rewriting/paraphrase and moderation workflows may introduce measurable changes such as refusal, omission, sanitization, semantic drift, lexical substitution, compression, uncertainty inflation, or loss of provenance context. The research is designed to measure where these effects occur, where they do not occur, and how to preserve reviewable evidence without forcing unsupported attribution.

`UNKNOWN / OUT OF DISTRIBUTION / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` remain valid outcomes.
