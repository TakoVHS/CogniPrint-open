# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CogniPrint is an open-source research project. We want participation in the project to be constructive, respectful, safe, and welcoming to people of different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas, code, research methods, and review comments respectfully;
- focus criticism on the work rather than the person;
- give reproducible, evidence-based technical feedback where practical;
- respect privacy, security, licensing, and research-governance boundaries;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and repository policy;
- help keep issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions useful to other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not accept harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, targeted personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted disclosure of private information, spam, or attempts to pressure contributors into bypassing security, privacy, licensing, or research-governance controls.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, review channels, project documentation spaces, and other public spaces used to represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you encounter conduct that violates this policy, contact the maintainer through the repository's available private contact channel or GitHub reporting tools when appropriate. Reports should include enough context to review the incident while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

## Good-faith review

Enforcement decisions should be made in good faith, consistently, and with attention to context. This policy is intended to support constructive open-source collaboration, not to suppress technical disagreement or rigorous scientific review.
